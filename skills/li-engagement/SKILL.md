# li-engagement

Read [the shared contract](../li-context/references/context-contract.md) first.

For requests to find new people/companies/posts on LinkedIn, apply the discovery
mode in [li-research](../li-research/SKILL.md) first. Search candidates are clues,
not relationships or verified profiles; do not open LinkedIn URLs or silently add
people to relationship memory. Deeper research needs a separate user request,
identity disambiguation and permitted non-LinkedIn sources or supplied snapshots.

Apply [li-read](../li-read/SKILL.md) to available network/public-post/thread data.
Inspect [attention guidance](../li-brief/references/attention.md), relationship
records and confirmed comment history. Relevant author alone is insufficient.
Consider expertise, projects, recency, fresh evidence, prior interactions and whether
there is a genuine contribution or explicit unfinished conversation. Verify public
claims via li-research only when necessary. Skip repetitive engagement and low-value
signals; an empty recommendation list is valid.

For each worthwhile candidate provide PERSON, POST/SIGNAL, WHY IT MATTERS,
RELATIONSHIP CONTEXT, WHY NOW, RECOMMENDED MANUAL ACTION and DRAFT where justified.
Use [li-comment](../li-comment/SKILL.md) or li-dm logic with public evidence and voice.
No generic praise, inferred sensitive traits or cold outreach without a substantive
reason. Do not enrich private contacts with invasive data or scan the entire network.
