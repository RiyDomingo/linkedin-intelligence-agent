# li-reply

Read [the shared context and approval contract](../li-context/references/context-contract.md) first.

Read the original post and supplied comments. Count and group LEAD, SUBSTANCE,
PEER, SUPPORT and NOISE; classify from content, not assumed commercial value.
Prioritize questions and substantive disagreement. Answer directly, concede valid
criticism, avoid inventing experience and do not pitch reflexively. Keep supportive
acknowledgements short and skip spam. Give copy-ready replies grouped by bucket,
with reasons for uncertain classifications. Apply li-fact-check and li-human.
Likes or moderation may be recommendations only; never operate the account.

For “Review the comments on my latest post”, first use [li-read](../li-read/SKILL.md)
to find the latest actually dated own post and exposed comments; otherwise fall back
to imported/supplied thread text. Consider relationship records, prior interactions,
projects and whether the thread is already answered. Missing comments are not proof
of an empty thread. NO RESPONSE NEEDED is valid; no generic supportive reply required.
