# li-weekly

Read [the shared contract](../li-context/references/context-contract.md) first.

Apply [li-read](../li-read/SKILL.md), preserving snapshot dates and capability gaps.
Run sibling li-read `scripts/intelligence.py --root <data-root> weekly`. Combine
confirmed history with available analytics, relationship records and current evidence;
imports remain read observations, not automatically confirmed publication history.
Apply li-audit reasoning to metrics and [attention guidance](../li-brief/references/attention.md)
to pending conversations and emerging signals. A blank metric is unknown, not zero.

Answer: what happened; what mattered; what we learned (observation versus hypothesis);
who deserves follow-up; repeated arguments/stories/companies; emerging opportunities;
and next week's priorities. Note dates, sample sizes, unavailable sources and stale
analytics. Relevant professional discussions and strategic importance matter more
than raw likes. Do not infer lead quality or meaningful comments without supplied
labels or actual discussion evidence. Use li-history and li-ideas for fresh angles,
then li-plan for a realistic next week. Keep the review selective and human-executed.
